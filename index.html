How the rules reach the reviewer

Every review needs the same rules: the writing checks, the JSON schema and your style guide if you picked one. That text is most of what each review costs. These modes differ only in how often it gets re-sent. Figures are measured over 24 paragraphs damaged identically and reviewed by all three, not estimates.

Changing anything here starts a new conversation, so the reviewer is never working from rules you have since changed.
